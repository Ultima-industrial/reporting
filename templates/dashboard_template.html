    Cash Flow Forecast — draft, 6 months

    Overdue receivables — yesterday is approximated from today's open-invoice snapshot (Odoo only exposes currently-open invoices, not a historical one), so it slightly undercounts anything paid since yesterday.

    Cash balance's dashed segment (next 60 days) is the same draft projection as the Cash Flow Forecast below, just zoomed into its first 60 days alongside 30 days of actual history for context.

    Cash flow forecast is a draft, 6 months ahead: it projects known open vendor bill and customer invoice due dates, configured import VAT prepayment rules and their quarterly recovery, estimated payment dates for confirmed purchase orders with no vendor bill yet (from their payment term + expected arrival date), and the configured December VAT acconto — against the current balance. No new sales, recurring costs, or fully realistic payment-timing behavior are modeled yet. Anything already overdue/due is assumed to land today rather than on its original due date.

    Import VAT recovery (suppliers based in a listed country, see config/import_vat_rules.yaml) is modeled as recovered at Ultima's next quarterly IVA settlement (16 May / 20 Aug / 16 Nov, or via the annual return the following 16 March for Q4) rather than the following month, since Ultima files quarterly, not monthly.

    Immediate Payment import POs (supplier based in a listed country, see config/import_vat_rules.yaml) are anchored 7 days before expected arrival rather than on the arrival date itself, since these suppliers require payment before dispatch. A domestic Immediate Payment PO still anchors on the expected arrival date.

    Confirmed orders awaiting invoice shows an estimated payment date computed from each purchase order's payment term ("termini di pagamento") plus its expected arrival date — an approximation until a real vendor bill exists, worth sanity-checking against a few real POs. Only the REMAINING un-invoiced balance is estimated here: if a PO has been partially invoiced (e.g. a "fattura acconto" already received), that invoice's own due date already shows under Open Vendor Bills, and only the still-unbilled remainder is estimated, using the same payment-term proportions applied to that smaller balance. A PO (or its remaining balance) missing a payment term, an expected arrival date, or using a payment-term schedule not yet supported is excluded here and flagged instead (see "Needs attention" above if any exist).

    Expected invoiced revenue by month assumes deliveries land on their promised date (commitment_date, "Data Consegna") and are invoiced immediately ("fatture immediate") — a delayed delivery shows in the month it was originally promised, not when it actually ships. Uses each order line's remaining amount to invoice (net of VAT), not the order's full value, so a partially-invoiced order only contributes what's left to invoice.

    Quotes raised — yesterday uses each order's current state, not its state as of yesterday — a quote raised yesterday but already confirmed into a sale order by today drops out of both figures rather than staying counted in yesterday's.

    New Orders (MTD) and Sales Won (MTD) count only confirmed sale orders (Odoo state “Ordine di vendita”) — draft/sent quotations (“Preventivo”) are excluded. Sales Won is order value (net of VAT), by order date, not invoiced revenue, so it can differ from Revenue (MTD, invoiced).

    Source: Odoo (sale orders, bank feed, open invoices/bills), read-only. Ask for a refresh any time to pull a new snapshot.
